Challenge 3: Coupon Redemption Prediction
Task Description
Predict whether a customer will redeem a coupon offer for a specific SKU. Using customer profiles, historical spending patterns, email engagement, and coupon characteristics, the model helps optimize marketing campaigns and promotional targeting to maximize redemption rates and ROI.

Evaluation Metric
Macro-F1 Score - Balances precision and recall for coupon redemption prediction, ensuring effective targeting while minimizing wasted promotional spend.
