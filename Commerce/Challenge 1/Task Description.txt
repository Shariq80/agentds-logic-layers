Challenge 1: Weekly Store-SKU Demand Forecasting
Task Description
Predict how many units will be sold next week for each SKU. Using historical sales data including weekly sales patterns, pricing, and promotional activities, the model helps retailers optimize inventory planning and avoid stockouts or overstocking. This forecasting enables better supply chain coordination and cost management.

Evaluation Metric
Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE) - Measures prediction accuracy for unit sales, with lower values indicating better forecasting performance.
