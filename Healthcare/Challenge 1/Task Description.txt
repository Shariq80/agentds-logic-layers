Challenge 1: 30-Day Readmission Prediction
Task Description
Predict whether an inpatient admission will be followed by a readmission within 30 days. This binary classification task uses admission data including length of stay, acuity level, Charlson comorbidity index, recent ED visits, and discharge timing to assess readmission risk.

Evaluation Metric
Macro-F1 Score - Balances precision and recall across readmission and non-readmission classes, ensuring the model performs well for both outcomes.
